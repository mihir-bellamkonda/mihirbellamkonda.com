The data glow nameless,
bruisey and extinguishing as
cigarette on skin.

We’ve talked about this.
I watch the skyscreen go again
black, purple, blue;

the ending colors.
Heat, extinctions, fascia.
Nothing refreshing alters.

But we’ve talked about this.
I brush soot from wound, wash,
apply your balm. Mint.
This stretched week
I have worried for
the name of the tree on the corner,

which the dog always sniffs
in a slow circle, then marks--
the tree with whispering seeds,

future-shaped,
all wing and fat fecundity.
Then

you kiss
the syllables
into my left ear:

*green ash*
