---
title: "Epiphany"
date: 2026-07-26
unpublished: true
---

In an empty state on an empty hill in an empty field on a empty plot
there’s an empty house in whose kitchen several stars appear. They flash.
You’re watching through a mirror. You are flashing.
There’s a message written in the light.
For a moment you have it, then
only afterimages, dancing amps in purple and green, lovely
and useless as pressing hard on closed eyes.
