---
title: "Old Man at Dinner"
date: 2025-12-01
unpublished: true
---

The waitress hands me a glass of something honey-colored
with a violet floating on the surface.

*No charge*, she says, her lips brushing my burning ear.
How can I understand?

Perhaps she is lonely, so she has concocted a gift for her polite regular,
slicing peaches to kill time.

Or perhaps I resemble her father, so she has decided to poison me.
You must forgive me these daydreams.

I remember men like me must ask forgiveness.

The drink tastes like chamomile and stonefruit. The ice melts softly.
She is far away now,

far away, across the small bright room, taking a rare order of the permanent special,
pancakes with cherry jelly.

The glass is warm. As I tip the last of it towards me, dreaming up meanings,
the violet curves off my lip

like a shy lover or the skin of boiled milk. For a long moment
I do nothing,

my face slack as a caught child’s, my loose mouth hanging purple-petaled and sweet;
I am forgetting

the many things I did wrong, the fewer things I put right,
the name on her apron.
