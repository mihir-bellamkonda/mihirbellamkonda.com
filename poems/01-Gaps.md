---
title: "Gaps"
date: 2019-12-01
catalogue: 25
unpublished: true
---

It’s an open question whether you’re here or not.
Or not, you’re beginning to think.
In a meeting with friends, you mention
the shapes birds leave behind in flight.
A few minutes later, you’ve run out of things to discuss.
You walk home. You’ve lost your bicycle.
Alongside a yellow wall, you forget
that you had a bicycle at all.
Your shoes feel empty. They must be
too large, like a breeze
emptied of birds.
