---
title: "The Carpenter"
date: 2025-06-01
unpublished: true
---

I have three children who I love very much.
I bring them cool water in the evening
And sit by them until they fall asleep.
I see the green night light against their ears
And cry, thinking: Seashells.
It is difficult for me to say more.
My worst fear is that one day
I will wake up and not love them,
That they will be only stock in the back. Even then,
I would hold them like any wood, with both hands.
