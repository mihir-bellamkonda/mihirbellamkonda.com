It’s useful like the underside
of a leaf: maybe

harbouring some thing jewel-red,
crawling and tiny, or
containing pleasure
like approaching
a downy skin,
a small of the back
with a series
of mean kisses.

But probably it’s almost nothing,
a half leaf let half go,
then wholly
into the street’s distance;
just something enough
to be brief with.

(The other side was darker.)
