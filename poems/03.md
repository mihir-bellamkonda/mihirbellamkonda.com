---
title: "Elegy for Small Things"
date: 2024-08-05
published_in: "The Atlantic"
external_url: "https://www.theatlantic.com/example"
---

A teacup, chipped at the rim.
The last page of a beloved book.
    A button that came loose
        from your favorite coat.

These small deaths
    accumulate like dust,
        barely noticed
            until you try to breathe.

But also: the first sip of morning coffee.
    The weight of a cat in your lap.
        Sunlight through lace curtains,
            making patterns on the floor.

*These small resurrections*,
    equally unnoticed,
        equally essential.

We live in the margins
    between loss and discovery,
        learning to love
            what remains.
