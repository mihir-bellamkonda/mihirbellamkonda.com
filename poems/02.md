---
title: "Untitled (After Rain)"
date: 2024-09-20
---

The streets glisten,
mirrors reflecting a sky
that has forgotten its tears.

Petrichor rises,
    that ancient scent of earth
    greeting water like an old friend.

Children splash through puddles,
their laughter echoing
against wet brick walls.

I breathe deep,
    tasting the clean air,
        remembering that even storms
            must end.
