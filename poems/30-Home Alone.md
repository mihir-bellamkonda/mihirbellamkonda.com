---
title: "Home Alone"
date: 2025-10-01
unpublished: true
---

You can sit on your couch as long as you want,
you can take slow sweet sips of clean water,
you can look out the window as long as you want,
petting the cat and then petting the air where the cat was,
look at your hands moving in the light of the kitchen,
you can do the dishes as long as you want,
washing and drying.
