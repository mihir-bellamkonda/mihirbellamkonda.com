---
title: "The Tailors"
date: 2025-04-01
unpublished: true
---

When it went wrong, we moved south
to a town with strange birds.
We began
to sell clothes.
I widened the thighs of jeans.
You fit the right hat to the right head.
We became known in the town
for the sadness of our smiles
and the durability of our hickory cloth,
with which we covered knees
and elbows.

We never got anywhere
but we had the apartment above the store,
enough for food, and I like to imagine
some heap in heaven of prevented scars.
At night we dressed up for each other.
Lost in the cottons and breaking silks.
When I imagined your navel,
I saw a perfect buttonhole,
Then I saw a perfect button.
