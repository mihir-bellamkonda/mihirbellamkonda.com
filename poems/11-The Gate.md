I'm brown as earth with an ॐ tattoo but I remember
the image. You've seen it: pearly gates, pearly man,
good or bad, in or out. Hey, it's pretty, I admit, clean,
something to look forward to. My mother said that
about the weekend and big meals, both of which
tire you out. Speaking of you, break my head open
again and ask me whether St. Peter looks forward
to anything. Or perhaps he’s content with his pearls?

Smearing nacreous doves against my vision and
the taste of cold brick or revenge served. Gasping maybe
my last in the alley. Alveoli and vision narrowing. Please,
if this is it, please: let me not pass through the pearls.
Let me drink them in vinegar like Cleopatra. Like Angelou,
let me dispense them from my dark throat. Please.
