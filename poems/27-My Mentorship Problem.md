title: "My Mentorship Problem"
date: 2025-04-01
unpublished: true
---

I wrote a poem about volcanoes. Not bad
he said, and lent me a textbook about them
which he promised would provide accurate details
for future work in the vein. I wrote a poem
about why I wrote a poem and he suggested
a more elegant conclusion, grounded, he noted,
in Goethe’s indispensable *Maximen und Reflexionen*.
I wrote a poem about love and he told me a story
from his youth involving a pair of Flemish twins,
three bottles of wine, and a very loose pair of pants.
So I wrote a poem about how I had invented poetry,
bread, sunlight, and cannibalism. I concluded with a line
wherein I ate him, feet first—his first time.
